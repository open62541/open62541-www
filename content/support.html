The EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)

Manufacturers placing products with digital components on the EU market become responsible for security, maintenance, documentation, and conformity of third-party components like open62541. Under the CRA, you need reliable processes, artifacts, and long-term maintenance commitments for the open-source components you ship.
